Before you move

  • Orlando International Airport (MCO) has direct flights to many U.S. and international cities.
  • Florida has no state personal income tax. Property taxes and homestead rules vary; ask your tax advisor.
  • Homeowners insurance and flood zones affect monthly costs. We'll help you check before you buy.

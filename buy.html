The buying process

Six steps to closing

  1. Consultation

    We talk through your budget, areas, must-haves and timeline, in person or by video.

  2. Financing

    Get pre-approved with a lender of your choice. We can share lenders and down-payment assistance resources. We are not a lender.

  3. Search and showings

    We send matching MLS listings and tour homes with you, in person or virtually.

  4. Offer

    Your agent prepares and submits your offer and represents you through negotiation.

  5. Inspection and appraisal

    Inspections, appraisal and loan approval happen during the contract period.

  6. Closing

    Sign at the title company, get your keys, and we stay in touch for the life of your home.

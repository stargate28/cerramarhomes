Orange County

Orlando

The region's center for jobs, entertainment and travel, with a downtown core and neighborhoods in every direction.

  • Home to Orlando International Airport (MCO)
  • Walt Disney World, Universal Orlando and SeaWorld are nearby
  • Downtown, Lake Eola Park and the Amway Center
  • Major routes: I-4, SR 408, SR 417 and Florida's Turnpike

Osceola County

Kissimmee

The Osceola County seat, just south of Orlando and next to the theme-park corridor.

  • On the shores of Lake Tohopekaliga (Lake Toho)
  • Minutes from Walt Disney World along US-192
  • Historic downtown Kissimmee and the lakefront park
  • Access to Florida's Turnpike and SR 417

Polk County

Davenport

A fast-growing area southwest of Orlando with many newer communities, including vacation-home neighborhoods.

  • Near the I-4 and US-27 interchange
  • Close to ChampionsGate and the western edge of the theme parks
  • Short drive to Haines City and Lakeland
  • A common choice for vacation and second homes

Osceola County

St. Cloud

A lakeside city east of Kissimmee with a historic downtown and room to grow.

  • On East Lake Tohopekaliga, with a lakefront park and marina
  • A historic downtown district
  • Connected to Orlando by Florida's Turnpike and US-192
  • Close to the Lake Nona area to the north

Lake County

Clermont

Known for rolling hills and a chain of lakes, west of Orlando.

  • On Lake Minneola, with the Clermont Waterfront Park
  • Home of the Florida Citrus Tower
  • A training destination for cyclists and triathletes
  • Connected by Florida's Turnpike and US-27

Orange County (southeast Orlando)

Lake Nona

A planned community in southeast Orlando built around health care, research and technology.

  • Lake Nona Medical City, including the UCF College of Medicine and a VA Medical Center
  • Home of the USTA National Campus
  • Minutes from Orlando International Airport
  • Access to SR 417 and Florida's Turnpike

Polk County

Winter Haven

The "Chain of Lakes City," midway between Orlando and Tampa.

  • Connected lakes linked by canals for boating
  • Home to LEGOLAND Florida Resort
  • Historic downtown Winter Haven
  • Reachable via US-27, US-17 and SR 60

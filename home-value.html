    How the estimate works

    1. County records

      Your home's size, age, lot and features from the county property appraiser, for Orange, Osceola, Polk, Lake and Seminole counties.

    2. Recent nearby sales

      Recorded arm's-length sales of similar homes in your neighborhood, adjusted for size, age and lot.

    3. Today's market

      Older sales are moved to today's prices with ZIP-code trends from Zillow Research and Redfin.

    Tested on 3,000 real 2026 Central Florida sales, half of the estimates were within 7.3% of the actual sale price. A computer can't see upgrades, condition or views, so our broker reviews every request.
